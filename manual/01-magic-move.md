# Magic Move

Magic Move animates matching objects from one slide into the next. Everything else fades out or in.

Select the first slide in the transition, make sure no object is selected, then find **Magic Move** under **Props**.

> Screenshot placeholder: The Magic Move controls in the Props sidebar.

### Auto-pair

Click **Enable and Auto-Pair**. DeckWerk enables the transition to the next slide and pairs objects it can match confidently, including related text, media, and shapes.

Auto-pair deliberately leaves uncertain objects alone. Open the Magic Move editor if a match is missing or wrong.

Select several consecutive slides before clicking **Enable Magic-Move & Auto-Pair** to apply it across the whole run.

> Screenshot placeholder: An auto-paired source and target slide.

### Manual pair

Click **Open Magic Move editor…**. Choose an object on the source slide, then choose its partner on the target slide. Matching numbers show the pairs.

Use **×** to remove one pair or **Clear pairs** to start over. Click **Done** when the transition is ready.

> Screenshot placeholder: Manually pairing two objects in the Magic Move editor.

Duration and motion curve apply to Magic Move throughout the deck.
