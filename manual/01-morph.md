# Morph

Morph animates matching objects from one slide into the next. Objects that are identical across slides don't change at all. Objects that are different, but not paired, fade out or in.

Select the first slide in the transition, then find **Morph** under **Props**.

> Screenshot placeholder: The Morph controls in the Props sidebar.

### Auto-pair

Click **Enable and Auto-Pair**. DeckWerk enables the transition to the next slide and pairs objects it can match confidently, including related text, media, and shapes.

Auto-pair deliberately leaves uncertain objects alone. Open the Morph editor if a match is missing or wrong.

Select several consecutive slides before clicking **Enable Morph & Auto-Pair** to apply it across the whole run.

> Screenshot placeholder: An auto-paired source and target slide.

### Manual pair

Click **Open Morph editor…**. Choose an object on the source slide, then choose its partner on the target slide. Matching numbers show the pairs.

Use **×** to remove one pair or **Clear pairs** to start over. Click **Done** when the transition is ready.

> Screenshot placeholder: Manually pairing two objects in the Morph editor.

Duration and motion curve apply to Morph throughout the deck.
